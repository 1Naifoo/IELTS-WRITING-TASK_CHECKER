# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be kind, patient and respectful. Many people here are learning English.
- Welcome people of every background, level of experience and language.
- Give feedback on ideas and code, not on people.
- No harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- No sharing of other people's private information.

## Reporting

If someone behaves in a way that breaks these rules, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub ([@1Naifoo](https://github.com/1Naifoo)). Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may remove comments, code or contributions that break this code, and may block people who repeatedly break it.
